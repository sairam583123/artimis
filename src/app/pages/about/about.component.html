About Us

Crafting trust, one fitting at a time

For over two decades we've manufactured taps, sanitaryware and bathroom fittings that builders, plumbers and homeowners rely on every day.

Our Story

From a small workshop to a nationwide brand

What began as a modest brass-casting workshop has grown into a full-scale manufacturing operation producing taps, sanitaryware, showers, valves and accessories. Along the way, our focus has never wavered: build fittings that perform reliably long after installation.

Today our products reach homes, hotels and commercial sites through a growing network of dealers and distributors, backed by an in-house quality lab that pressure-tests every batch before it ships.
